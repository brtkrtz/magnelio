# `magnelio.ports`

`PortWaveguide.from_face(face, model=..., name=...)` resolves a singular
rectangular `FaceRef` on a PEC domain face into the ordinary declarative
port. See [selected EM faces](../methods/geometry.md) for eligibility and
ownership rules.
